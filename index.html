Equidistribution on the Torus

Fall 2025 · MATH 245A Real Analysis · Instructor: Terence Tao

A ten-page expository project on equidistribution of irrational rotations on the circle. The work uses exponential sums, finite geometric series, and approximation by trigonometric polynomials, with an extension to quadratic sequences using Weyl differencing and van der Corput's inequality.

Polymath Jr. — Finding Ellipses

Summer 2026 · Collaborative Undergraduate Mathematics Program

During Summer 2026, I participated in the Polymath Jr. collaborative mathematics program, studying the first seven chapters of Finding Ellipses: What Blaschke Products, Poncelet's Theorem, and the Numerical Range Know about Each Other by Daepp, Gorkin, Shaffer, and Voss.

The project introduced me to several interconnected areas of mathematics, including complex analysis, geometry, and operator theory. I studied foundational results such as the Schwarz lemma, the maximum modulus principle, and automorphisms of the unit disk, followed by finite Blaschke products and their connections to ellipses. The later chapters introduced Poncelet's theorem for triangles and the numerical range of linear operators.

As supplementary reading, I studied parts of Joel H. Shapiro's Notes on the Numerical Range, which introduced me to the elliptical range theorem for 2×2 matrices and the Toeplitz–Hausdorff theorem. Although some of the operator arguments remained challenging, the project motivated me to explore connections between complex analysis, linear algebra, and geometry more deeply.

Reference: Finding Ellipses by Daepp, Gorkin, Shaffer, and Voss.
